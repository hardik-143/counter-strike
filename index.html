  • Economy. Start with $800. Win a round: $3250 ($3500 for bomb or defuse). Lose: $1400, +$500 per loss in a row. Kill: $300.
  • Accuracy. Stand still or crouch to shoot straight. Tap or burst at range; recoil climbs while spraying.
  • Cover. Waist-high crates block bullets: crouch behind them, peek over them standing.
  • Bomb. Carry it to site A or B, press 5 and hold fire for 3 seconds. It detonates after 35 seconds.
  • Defuse. Hold E on the bomb: 10 seconds, or 5 with a defuse kit.
  • Utility. Smoke blocks vision and puts out incendiary fire. Flashbangs blind anyone facing them.
