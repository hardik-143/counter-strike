LAST FUSE

Tactical bomb-defusal shooter · 5v5 with bots · map: Sandline

MULTIPLAYER

Online: one player creates a room and shares the 5-letter code or link. Works over the internet, no install.

Controls & rules
  • WASD move · Mouse aim · LMB fire · RMB scope / knife stab
  • C or Ctrl crouch · Shift walk (silent) · Space jump
  • 1 primary · 2 pistol · 3 knife · 4 grenades · 5 bomb
  • R reload · Q last weapon · G drop · E use / defuse / pick up
  • B buy menu · Tab scoreboard · Esc pause
  • Raiders win by detonating the bomb or eliminating the Wardens.
  • Wardens win by defusing, eliminating the Raiders, or running out the clock.
  • Start with $800. Win: $3250 (bomb/defuse $3500). Loss: $1400, +$500 per consecutive loss. Kill: $300.
  • Stand still / crouch to shoot accurately. Tap or burst at range; recoil climbs while spraying.
  • Waist-high crates are cover: crouch behind them, peek over them standing.

Original game inspired by classic tactical team shooters. All graphics and sound are generated in code.
